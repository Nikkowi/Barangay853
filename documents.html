🔷 Barangay Clearances
💰 Fee: ₱50.00 📅 3–5 working days 🕗 Mon–Fri, 8AM–5PM
📋 What to Bring
  • ✓ Valid Government-issued ID
  • ✓ Proof of Address (utility bill or lease contract)
  • ✓ 1×1 ID Picture — 2 copies
  • ✓ Cedula or Community Tax Certificate
  • ✓ Processing fee of ₱50.00
🪜 How to Apply — Step by Step
  • 1 Go to the Barangay Hall and proceed to the Secretary's window
  • 2 Fill out the request form and submit your documents
  • 3 Pay the processing fee at the cashier
  • 4 Wait for your name to be called or return on the release date
